Frequently Asked Questions

Does compression reduce image quality?
Moderate compression (70%–85% quality) produces virtually indistinguishable results while reducing file size by 50%–80%. You can freely adjust the quality parameter based on your needs.
Are my images uploaded anywhere?
No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.
What image formats are supported?
We support JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and GIF formats. After compression, you can choose to output as JPG, PNG, or WebP.
How many images can I compress at once?
You can compress up to 50 images at once. For more images, simply process them in batches.
How well does PNG compression work?
PNG is a lossless format, so browser-side compression is limited. For better results, consider converting PNG to WebP or JPG (if transparency is not needed).
